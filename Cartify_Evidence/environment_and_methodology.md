## SDLC and methodology

Cartify followed an **iterative, feature-driven** model:

1. Requirements, feasibility and MoSCoW prioritization
2. Architecture, Flutter setup and Hive models
3. Authentication and profile persistence
4. Firestore catalogue and product discovery
5. Cart, wishlist, checkout and orders
6. Administrator panel and product management
7. Testing, academic evidence and documentation

The repository was managed with Git and GitHub:

`https://github.com/N1VD55P/quickcart`

The recorded project period is approximately four months, from 19 July 2026
to 16 November 2026. These are feature phases, not formally numbered Scrum
sprints.
